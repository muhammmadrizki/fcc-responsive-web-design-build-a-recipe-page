# Special Indomie Goreng Recipe

A simple and delicious Indomie Goreng recipe with egg, sausage, and fresh mustard greens (caisim). Perfect for a quick and satisfying meal.

## Ingredients

- 1 pack of Indomie Goreng
- 1 egg
- 2 sausages, sliced
- 1 handful of mustard greens (caisim / sawi hijau)
- Water for boiling
- Cooking oil

## Instructions

1. Boil water in a pot and cook the Indomie noodles until tender. Drain the water.
2. Heat cooking oil in a pan, then fry the sliced sausages until cooked.
3. Add the egg and mustard greens. Stir-fry until the egg is cooked and the vegetables are slightly wilted.
4. Add the Indomie noodles and all seasoning packets. Mix everything well, then serve hot.

## Recipe Information

- **Recipe Name:** Special Indomie Goreng
- **Preparation:** Easy
- **Cooking Steps:** 4
- **Main Ingredients:** Indomie Goreng, egg, sausage, and mustard greens
